Carolyn Seiler & Friends

An artist co-op in the heart of Cocoa Village. Browse work by more than 30 local artists, chat with the people who made it, and take home something you won't find anywhere else.

Something for every wall, shelf and wrist

Every artist in the co-op makes their own work, and you'll often find one of them minding the shop. Here's what's in the building.

  • Paintings
  • Ceramics
  • Glasswork
  • Jewelry
  • Photography
  • Mixed media
  • Prints
  • Gifts & ornaments
You can't miss it

Look for the big blue building

Yellow awnings, a painted pole out front and an OPEN sign in neon. Visitors are encouraged to browse, chat with the artists and soak up the colorful, collaborative spirit that makes this place a Cocoa Village favorite.
